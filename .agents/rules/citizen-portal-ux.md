# Citizen Portal UX & Dashboard Design Standards

1. **Visual Continuity (Login to Home)**: Maintain the same clean aesthetic, typography (Inter), palette (CiDi primary blue, cyan accent, navy text, light slate background), and topbar identity between the pre-login portal and the authenticated citizen home.
2. **Top-Level Navigation over Sidebars**: For general public and citizen portals, prefer an institutional topbar + horizontal subnavigation bar over dark or dense vertical sidebars. This optimizes reading width, prevents visual clutter, and delivers superior mobile ergonomics.
3. **Personalized Identity Hero**: The authenticated home should welcome the citizen clearly with their first name, verification credential (e.g., CUIL, Level 2 Verified badge), and an instant service search bar.
4. **Primary Quick Actions**: Elevate the 4 core citizen operations (Initiate Procedure, Pay Taxes, Digital File, Citizen Hotline 147) as high-contrast, card-based touchpoints directly accessible above the fold.
5. **Two-Column Dashboard Hierarchy**: Organize desktop content with the citizen's active administrative workflow on the primary column (procedure tracking table, frequent services) and auxiliary contextual items on the secondary column (citizen file status, institutional news, special campaigns).
